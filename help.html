Help Centre

Need help using Quiz-Race? Find answers to common questions below.

Open the Rooms page and select an available room. Follow the instructions shown on the screen to join.

Open the Upload page, choose your PDF learning material, and follow the instructions to continue.

Select a quiz room or use the quiz option available after uploading your learning material. Follow the on-screen instructions to begin.

Visit the Leaderboard page to see rankings and available scores.

Open the Profile page from the bottom navigation. You can edit your profile information there.

Open Menu and select App Settings. You can change your appearance and other preferences there.

Still need help?

Contact the Quiz-Race team for further assistance.
