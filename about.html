About Quiz-Race

Making learning more fun, interactive, and competitive.

Who We Are

Quiz-Race is a learning platform designed to help students learn through fun quizzes and friendly competition. Our goal is to make studying more engaging and encourage students to test their knowledge.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make learning enjoyable and accessible by combining education with interactive quizzes, competition, and collaboration.

What Quiz-Race Offers

Quiz Rooms

Learn and compete with other players.

Learning Materials

Upload study materials to support your learning.

Leaderboard

Track rankings and celebrate achievements.

User Profiles

Manage your personal account information.
